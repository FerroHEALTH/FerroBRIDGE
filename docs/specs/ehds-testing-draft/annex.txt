                        Functional requirements of the European digital testing environment


 GEN       Generic assessment scope

 GEN-010   The European digital testing environment (‘EU DTE’) shall create logically separated workspaces for each testing
           organisation, to enable the testing of systems under test belonging to each such testing organisation.

 GEN-020   The EU DTE shall allow testing organisations to test specific versions of their systems.

 GEN-030   The EU DTE shall implement the latest technical specifications based on the requirements of Regulation (EU) 2025/327 and
           its implementing acts applicable to the testing process without any alteration of their content.

 GEN-040   The EU DTE shall provide an effective version control system to be used across the testing process. The version control
           system shall support the versioning of the following:
              •   digital testing environment;
              •   system under test;
              •   technical specifications.
           The version identifiers implemented by the version control system shall be complemented by the reference to the deployment
           environment lifecycle of systems under test, e.g. playground/development, pre-release, acceptance. The system under test
           shall not be tested in its production environment. The EU DTE shall not be used with real personal electronic health data.

 GEN-050   Test cases integrated in the EU DTE shall include clear references to the requirements of Regulation (EU) 2025/327 and
           those of implementing acts adopted on the basis of that Regulation with which conformity is assessed, such as [OP please add
           the number to the implementing act adopted pursuant to Article 36 of Regulation (EU) 2025/327]

 GEN-060   The EU DTE shall be maintainable and extensible to support updates of the requirements of Implementing Regulation
           (EU)…[OP please add the number of the implementing act under Article 15 of Regulation (EU) 2025/327], any related
           implementation guides adopted by the ‘EHDS Board’, the requirements of implementing act to be adopted pursuant to Article

 USR       User roles and permissions

 USR-010   The EU DTE shall allow at least the following roles to be created, read, updated, and deleted:
           -   system administrator, which concerns the environment provider side, e.g. Member State entities);
           -   testing organisation administrator, which concerns the testing environment user side, e.g. ‘EHR system’ manufacturers);
           -   tester.

 USR-020   The system administrator shall be able to:
               a) configure system settings (see SET) including the qualified electronic seal certificate;
               b) register and manage test domains;
               c) register and manage details of technical specifications used for testing;
               d) register and manage test suites for each technical specification;
               e) create, read, update, and delete other system administrators;
               f) create, read, update, and delete the testing organisations;
               g) create, read, update, and delete the relation between the testing organisations and testing organisation administrators;
               h) configure validators and simulators
               i) monitor the test history of testing organisations;
               j) access the audit trail of testing organisations;
               k) access the audit trail of the EU DTE.

 USR-030   The testing organisation administrator, within the organisation, shall be able to:
               a) create, read, update, and delete systems under test (name, version, type);
               b) create, read, update, and delete conformity statements;
               c) create, read, update, and delete other testing organisation administrators;
               d) create, read, update, and delete testers;
               e) request and download the conformity report;
               f) generate API keys for automatic testing;

               g) monitor test history of the testing organisation.
               h) configure the test history sharing rights;
               i) access audit trail of the organisation workspace.

 USR-040    The tester shall be able to:
               a) execute assigned test cases;
               b) request and download the conformity report;
               c) monitor testing progress, including its history;
               d) access the audit trail of test sessions performed by the tester.

 USR-050    The system administrator shall be able to configure at least the following test domains and test specifications details:
               a) its name;
               b) its version;
               c) one or more priority categories of personal electronic health data referred to in Article 14(1) of Regulation (EU)
                  2025/327, or their main characteristics thereof, to which the technical specification is applicable;
               d) test suites and associated test cases (read-only);
               e) the validator and/or the simulator to be used, if applicable.

 USR-060    The testing organisation administrator shall be able to specify in the conformity statement details:
               a) the system under test, including its version and the classification based on implementing act to be adopted pursuant to
                  Article 36 of Regulation (EU) 2025/327;
               b) the technical specifications, compliance with which is claimed for the system under test.

 USR-1070   The EU DTE users shall be able to manage their user profile and information on the basis of their authorisations.

 USR-080    The EU DTE shall support an automatic user logout after a period of inactivity.

 SET        Configuration settings

 SET-010    The EU DTE shall allow configuration settings to be managed, such as system properties, supported login mechanisms,
            landing page, legal notice, error templates, themes, retention period for testing history, timeout for the automatic user logout,


EN                                                            2                                                                            EN
           and help pages.

 SET-020   The EU DTE shall enable the configuration of the secure API, such as a REST API. The API configuration shall include,
           where appropriate, enabling or disabling asynchronous testing, setting usage limits, and the definition of endpoint details.

 SET-030   The EU DTE shall support the update of technical specification details and related validators and simulators without the need
           to re-deploy the EU DTE.

 TST       Test execution

 TST-010   The EU DTE shall assign to EHR system manufacturer, on the basis of the conformity statement for the system under test
           and depending on the EHR system classification pursuant to implementing act to be adopted pursuant to Article 36 of
           Regulation (EU) 2025/327, the test cases to be executed by the system under test.

 TST-020   The EU DTE shall support the testing process of the system under test regardless of its maturity and readiness level.

 TST-030   The EU DTE shall support the import and export of common test suites in an open, standardised machine-readable format.
           The test cases shall be read-only and shall not be modified.

 TST-040   The EU DTE shall support the execution of different types of predefined test cases relevant to the self-assessment of
           harmonised software components of EHR systems (e.g. data content-related validation, data exchange validation and
           requirements-related check list validation).

 TST-050   The EU DTE shall enable the manual validation of content through the upload of files and manual download of validation
           results.

 TST-060   The EU DTE shall support the execution of test cases by different means such as:
              a) validating content;
              b) filling in a checklist;
              c) sending or receiving data.




EN                                                          3                                                                         EN
 TST-070   The EU DTE shall enable the validation of the content for each priority category, including data groups, of personal
           electronic health data referred to in Article 14(1) of Regulation (EU) 2025/327) at document level, at resource level or both.

 TST-080   The EU DTE shall perform data content validation at different levels (syntax and semantic), including validation rules that
           consider interdependencies between data element values. The validation shall be performed on the basis of the data fields,
           value sets and coding systems set out in Implementing Regulation (EU) [OP please add the number of the Implementing
           Regulation adopted further to Article 15 of Regulation (EU) 2025/327]

 TST-090   The EU DTE shall enable users to access and monitor test step results and test results when running a test session.

 TST-100   The EU DTE shall support incremental testing of the system under test by one of the following:
              a) providing a list of all test results of the different versions of the same system under test;
              b) providing a list of all test results of the same version of the system under test using different versions of technical
                 specifications.

 TST-110   The EU DTE shall ensure that each test session is executed in isolation and not affected by any other test session. The test
           session shall be reproducible under the same conditions.

 TST-120   The EU DTE shall support the parallel and sequential execution of test sessions.

 TST-130   In addition to the on-demand real-time execution of test sessions the EU DTE shall support the asynchronous execution of
           test sessions.
           Testers shall be able to submit test executions, monitor their status, retrieve their results once completed, and cancel pending
           or running executions.

 TST-140   The EU DTE shall provide users with synthetic test data, including positive and negative examples for each test case, to
           enable the validation of the correct behaviours of the associated test cases in the testing environment.

 REP       Reporting




EN                                                           4                                                                            EN
 REP-010   Each test result produced by the EU DTE shall be assigned with one of the following statuses:
              •   SUCCESS when all the steps defined in the test case have been completed successfully following the test logic;
              •   FAILURE when at least one step defined in the test case has failed due to existing errors;
              •   UNDEFINED when the test case has either not been fully executed or was interrupted before completion and need to
                  be re-executed.
           The test result shall provide a list of any warnings and errors, that occurred during the testing including the pass/fail criteria
           defined for each test case. For check-list based testing, if an item on the checklist is not answered, the result status is
           UNDEFINED.

 REP-020   The EU DTE shall automatically generate a conformity report when the test session is completed. The conformity report shall
           contain at least the following:
              a) the EU DTE version including details of the technical specification, the test suites and the validation configuration;
              b) the operator;
              c) the details of the system under test;
              d) the testing organisation;
              e) the conformity statement;
              f) a list of all test cases executed to confirm the correctness of the conformity statement;
              g) the recorded start and end times and test results for all executed test sessions.

 REP-030   The EU DTE shall generate, at the user’s request, the conformity certificates according to a common template where all
           required test cases have been executed and have the result status ‘SUCCESS’. The conformity certificate shall contain at
           least:
              a) the certificate identifier;
              b) the date of issuance;
              c) the EU DTE version including details of the technical specification, the test suites and the validation configuration;
              d) the operator;
              e) the details of the system under test;
              f) the testing organisation;
              g) the conformity statement;



EN                                                            5                                                                            EN
              h) a list of all test cases executed to confirm the correctness of the conformity statement;
              i) the recorded start and end times and test results for all executed test sessions.
              j) a statement that all the test cases, executed to confirm the correctness of the conformity statement, have the result
                 status ‘SUCCESS’.

 REP-040   The EU DTE shall provide a mechanism for ensuring the integrity and authenticity of conformity certificates. Each
           conformity certificate shall be uniquely identified and electronically sealed with a qualified electronic seal of the operator
           pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 910/2014.

 REP-050   The EU DTE shall enable the provision of other conformity certificates that are clearly distinguished from the conformity
           certificate for the requirements set out in Anex II to Regulation (EU) 2025/327 for additional test cases added by Member
           States through national extensions. Such extensions may be used by Member States to verify compliance with national
           requirements.

 REP-060   The EU DTE shall maintain a registry of conformity certificates. The registry shall contain the minimum information to
           identify the authenticity of the conformity certificate, e.g. date and tested requirements.

 REP-070   The conformity certificates provided by the EU DTE shall be structured and exportable in both human-readable (e.g. PDF)
           and machine-readable formats. The machine-readable format shall be open and standardised. The conformity certificate shall
           be provided at least in English

 REP-080   The EU DTE shall support the automatic notification for test failures.

 REP-090   The EU DTE shall report test results in an open, standardised machine-readable format.

 SIT       System integration

 SIT-010   The EU DTE shall enable the testing organisation to generate for each conformity certificate issued, a public URI/URL
           through which the conformity certificate shall be accessible by external systems.

 SIT-020   The architecture of the EU DTE reference software shall be modular, providing the possibility to add or extend functionalities
           allowing Member States to use that reference software to assess the conformity of other aspects than the harmonised software



EN                                                          6                                                                          EN
           components with national requirements or to easily integrate the reference software in their existing national testing systems
           for the assessment of the harmonised software components.


 SIT-030   The EU DTE shall offer a validation API for the automatic validation of content through the machine-to-machine interaction
           for submitting files and receiving validation results.

 SIT-040   The EU DTE shall securely identify the user of the validation API, using a reliable mechanism.

 SIT-050   The EU DTE shall allow for the integration of the simulators for all content transmission APIs referred to in Annex I to
           Implementing Regulation (EU) [OP please add the number of the implementing act adopted under Article 15 of Regulation
           (EU) 2025/327]. The simulators shall include client and server simulators functionalities.

 SIT-060   In the simulators, the EU DTE shall securely identify the user of the simulator, such as a system under test or a continuous
           integration pipeline used to develop it, using a reliable mechanism such as API keys or public-key cryptography.

 SIT-070   The EU DTE shall include validators and simulators as distinct modules.

 SIT-080   The EU DTE shall display to its users its version and the level of the environment in which the EU DTE is operated, e.g.
           development, pre-release, acceptance, production, playground for demonstration and training purposes.

                                               Non-functional requirements of the European digital testing environment

     PRF             Performance

     PRF-010         The EU DTE shall support concurrent test sessions by different users without any significant impact on its performance.

     PRF-020         The EU DTE shall perform according to measurable key performance indicators defined by the EHDS Board.

     PRF-030         The EU DTE shall produce analytics of usage, including the numbers of test sessions performed and related test results, execution
                     timing.

     SEC             Security

     SEC-010         The EU DTE shall implement security measures to protect data and system integrity against unauthorised access, data alteration and
                     misuse in compliance with the relevant legislation such as Directive (EU) 2022/2555 of the European Parliament and of the Council1,
                     Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council 2,and relevant international standards.

     SEC-020         Access to the EU DTE shall be restricted to electronically identified, authenticated and authorised users. Personal data required for
                     authorisation and authentication process shall be limited to the EU DTE scope in accordance with the principle of data minimisation.

     SEC-030         The EU DTE shall enable secure login using strong user authentication as defined in Article 3(51) of Regulation (EU) No 910/2014,
                     including using European Digital Identity Wallets and European Business Wallets established pursuant to [OP: Please add Business
                     Wallets Regulation. It shall also support alternative means of strong user authentication.

     USB-050   The users shall be able to access clear information on the scope, status and outcome(s) of their testing activities. This shall include as a
               minimum: the tested requirement, a link to the technical specification from which the requirement stems, test steps, and the expected and

     USB-060   Error messages and warnings provided during the test execution shall be fit for purpose and actionable for the EU DTE users.
               Error messages and warnings shall clearly identify the specific requirement being violated and, where possible, remediation guidance.

     USB-070   The EU DTE shall include a functionality to display an advance notice of an upcoming update that might affect an ongoing testing

     USB-080   The EU DTE shall provide contextual user guidance, at least in English, using user-friendly mechanisms such as tooltips and info boxes.

     USB-100   The EU DTE shall facilitate the onboarding of new testing organisations and users through minimal manual steps.

     RLB       Reliability

     RLB-010   The EU DTE shall have measures in place to prevent the loss or corruption of test sessions and associated test results.

     AUD       Auditability

     AUD-010   The EU DTE shall keep a secure and immutable audit trail of all user accesses and performed test sessions.
               The audit trail shall include the following information on accesses:
                  a) user identifier;
                  b) time of access.
               The audit trail shall include the following information on all test sessions:
                  a) system under test;



EN                                              10                                                EN
                  b) test session identifier, start and end time, and test results;
                  c) test session status (complete, incomplete, interrupted, re-run);
                  d) information on whether manual or automatic validation of content was used.

     AUD-020   The EU DTE shall ensure that the audit trail is accessible by the authorised users immediately after the logged event has taken place.

     SCL       Scalability and portability

     SCL-010   The EU DTE shall be scalable in terms of compute capacity and storage resources to accommodate an increasing number of users, test
               cases and systems under test over time, including where these run in parallel.

     SCL-020   The EU DTE reference software shall use containerisation, e.g. Docker and Kubernetes, to minimise environment-specific dependencies
               when deployed..

     MNT       Maintainability

     MNT-010   Due to the availability of new technical specification and associated test cases, the EU DTE updates shall not invalidate previously
               generated conformity certificates.

     MNT-020   Following the introduction of support for a new version of a technical specification and associated test cases, the EU DTE shall maintain
               support for the previous versions of the technical specification and associated test cases, for the purposes of backward compatibility and
               reproducibility of test results. The default testing version shall be the most recent version of the relevant technical specification and
               associated test cases. There shall be a transition period during which the previous and new version can both be considered valid for
               performing the testing and obtaining a conformity certificate. The old version of the technical specification shall be discontinued when
               declared deprecated by the EHDS Board.

     MNT-030   Changes to the EU DTE shall be described in the maintenance plan and be versioned and duly documented in the release note providing
               the change request reference and the associated reason.

     DOC       Documentation

     DOC-010   The EU DTE documentation shall be made publicly available at least in English and kept up to date whenever a change is made to the
