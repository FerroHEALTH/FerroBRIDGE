- The outbound FHIR lane commits its delivery record with its watermark. Once the endpoint accepts a transaction Bundle, the lane stages what each composition stands at, and the follower writes the record (`outbound_delivered` in the state store) in the transaction that moves the lane's watermark under its lease generation, so a lane stopped before or after the endpoint answers sends the same idempotent Bundle again and a lane stopped after the commit sends nothing again: each effect lands once at the endpoint. A retry of a parked event is settled in the same transaction.
