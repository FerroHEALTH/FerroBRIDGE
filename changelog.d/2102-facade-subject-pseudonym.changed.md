- The FHIR facade keys every EHR by the patient's ITI-83 pseudonym, asked of the new top-level `[identity_source]` (a PIXm Manager such as FerroPIX: `endpoint`, `pseudonym_domain` and the ordered `source_domains`) by `POST`, so no direct identifier reaches the CDR or a URL. A subject named by identifier, and a `Patient`, is asked for by its identifier in the first accepted domain it carries. The `Patient` logical id is the party's `versioned_object_uid` where a `Patient` party context is loaded, which the state store records against the pseudonym, and the pseudonym elsewhere. A failed question answers `422` when the identity source holds no single pseudonym, `503` when a repeat may succeed and `502` otherwise, each naming the upstream status. An enabled facade refuses to start without `[identity_source]`.
