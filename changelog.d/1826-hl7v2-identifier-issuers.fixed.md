- An HL7 v2 composition names its health care facility and its composer by identifiers that carry their issuer, so a CDR that checks the facilities and the professional of the bridge's relay client (FerroEHR 5.7.0) admits it. A facility's namespace id (HD.1) is issued by the new `[hl7v2] facility_identifier_issuer`, a universal id (HD.2) by its type (HD.3), and an ordering facility's identifier (XON.10) by its assigning authority (XON.6). The clinician's id (XCN.1) is a composer identifier issued by its assigning authority (XCN.9) and typed by XCN.13, and a device sender's device id one issued by its `device_identifier_issuer`. In `fhirconnect`, `Defaults::with_composer_identifiers` writes a default composer with its identifiers whole as `composer|raw`.
