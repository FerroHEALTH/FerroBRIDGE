- An MLLP route bound to both a `certificate_subject` and `source_cidrs` now needs a connection that proves both: on the TLS listener the client certificate must carry the subject and the peer address must lie in one of the networks, and the plain listener refuses such a route, since a plain connection carries no certificate. A route bound to one of the two reads and is checked as before. Upgrade note: a route that names both so that one sender can reach either listener splits into two senders, or drops the proof it does not need. A webhook source refuses the new `network` verifier kind, which binds MLLP routes only.
