- `fhirconnect`: a `slotArchetype` may name a model already on its slot chain, as the mapping library's medication cluster does for its constituents. The compiler follows the recursion while the template carries a node, ends it where the template stops nesting the chain's archetype, and refuses a chain that enters one model more than `resolve::program::SLOT_DEPTH_LIMIT` (8) times or re-enters it at no deeper place. The diagnostic `fc-slot-cycle` is replaced by `fc-slot-depth`, and `EngineError::SlotCycle` by `EngineError::SlotDepth`, which carries the limit: a consumer that matches either changes to the new name.
