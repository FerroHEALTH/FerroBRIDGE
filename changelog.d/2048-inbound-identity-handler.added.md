- The inbound workers start on every replica that runs both `[inbound]` and the FHIR facade, and apply each stored item through one handler. An upsert reads the source's patient, keeps only its identifiers, names, birth date, gender and deceased status with the tenant's local identifier, feeds it to the identity source, asks ITI-83 for its pseudonym and ensures exactly one EHR for it. A remove marks the patient inactive at the identity source and never deletes an EHR. A source that declares no resolution parks its items as `resolution-undeclared`, and every upsert parks as `feed-client-unavailable` until a published client speaks ITI-104.
