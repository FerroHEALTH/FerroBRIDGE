- The HL7 v2 face holds the answer to each PID-3 assigning authority's identifier `NamingSystem` search across messages for `[hl7v2] authority_max_age`, 10 minutes by default, so a steady feed searches the terminology server once per authority in that period; a refused or unreachable search is never held. In `ferrobridge-hl7v2` 0.40.0, `map::map`, `map::single::segment` and `map::inverse::segment` take a `map::terminology::Terminology` (the client with the cache, bounded by a max age and a capacity) in place of the bare terminology client.
