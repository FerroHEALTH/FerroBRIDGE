- The bridge keeps its own state in a PostgreSQL state store that every replica shares, configured by the new `[state]` section (`url` or `url_file`, `tls_ca` or `tls_ca_file` under the `sslmode` rules of `[cdm]`, `max_connections`, default 10, and `acquire_timeout`, default `5s`) and kept apart from the OMOP CDM database. `serve` opens the store at start, refuses a server older than PostgreSQL 19, and applies the migrations the binary carries under an advisory lock, so replicas that start together migrate once; readiness gains a `state` indicator. `[state.seal]` lists the keys stored bodies are sealed under (`[[state.seal.keys]]` with `id` and `key` or `key_file`, the first one sealing). The store gives a single-consumer lane one leader across replicas through a lease with a fencing generation that refuses a former leader's cursor write, and carries in-memory copies on one `LISTEN` session per replica. Upgrade note: nothing moves yet, and the `redb` files stay in use; each later area that moves onto the store names the keys it removes.
