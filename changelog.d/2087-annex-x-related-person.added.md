A create of a party with relationships commits the party, then a second version with the relationships sourced at the uid the CDR assigned; when that second write fails, the facade answers the CDR's refusal with a warning that the party stands without its relationships. An update sources them before its one write. `fhirconnect`'s `engine::rm::PARTY_ROOTS` is `PARTY_REFS`.
