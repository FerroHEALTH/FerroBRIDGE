- `fhirconnect` and `openehr-mapping-core`: a composition model (`COMPOSITION.report.v1.Condition` and its kin) can be the `start` of a context compiled against an ADL 2 operational template. The template root carries every archetype its lineage names (AOM2 §3.6.4, §3.6.6), which `WebTemplateIndex` keeps from the OPT2's `parent_archetype_id`, or from `WebTemplateIndex::with_root_lineage` when the caller holds the template source and the OPT states no parent. `WebTemplateIndex::root_lineage` reads it. An OPT2 that states no parent and is given no lineage still refuses a composition start as an unresolved archetype root.
