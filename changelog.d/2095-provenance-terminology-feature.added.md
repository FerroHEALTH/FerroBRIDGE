- Breaking: `fhir-types` 0.2.0 generates the `Provenance` model and its schema behind the `terminology` feature in every FHIR version, so a terminology server reads and serves the provenance of a ConceptMap without the full resource set; each version's `resource::Resource` gains its `Provenance` variant under `terminology`, which breaks an exhaustive match. The same release generates the `r6` module from `hl7.fhir.r6.core` 6.0.0-snapshot1 in place of 6.0.0-ballot5 (#2072): the package drops `Contract`, `EnrollmentRequest`, `EnrollmentResponse`, `InsurancePlan`, `InsuranceProduct`, `Invoice` and `StructureMap` and the `MonetaryComponent` data type, and their `fhir_types::r6` modules go with them; a consumer that names one of them under `r6` moves to R5 or drops the use. The R6 examples corpus follows the snapshot's examples package and passes all 2321 resources, and `ferrobridge-term` reads and writes R6 as before. The crates built on `fhir-types` move a minor with it: `eehrxf` 0.28.0, `ferrobridge-fhirpath` 0.3.0, `ferrobridge-hl7v2` 0.28.0, `ferrobridge-term` 0.19.0, `ferrobridge-validator` 0.20.0 and `fhirconnect` 0.8.0.
