- `eehrxf` maps an imaging report between openEHR and the HL7 Europe Imaging Report 1.0.0-ballot document. The new `IMAGING` project (feature `imaging` with `openehr`) maps `OBSERVATION.imaging_exam_result.v1` to `DiagnosticReportEuImaging` and the `ImagingStudyEuImaging` it references, over FerroBRIDGE's ADL 2 imaging report template of published CKM archetypes. The modality and the study date, which the archetype holds in its event, are accounted losses. The testkit vendors the archetype through `scripts/vendor/ckm-imaging.sh`, and the EHDS readiness matrix lists both profiles.
