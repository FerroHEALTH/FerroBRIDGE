- The European interoperability component writes the imaging study manifest of draft EEHRxF Annex VII (initiative 17512, 2026-11-23) as an HL7 Europe Imaging Manifest 1.0.0-ballot `EuMadoBundle`: the `EuMadoImagingStudy` with its series, instances and WADO-RS `EuMadoWadoEndpoint`, the patient and the creator organization and device. The study comes from the imaging report mapping. The series, instances and services come from the caller, read from the image archive, and a manifest without them is refused (`eehrxf::document::manifest`). `hl7.fhir.eu.imaging-manifest` 1.0.0-ballot, `ihe.rad.mado` 1.0.0 and `ihe.formatcode.fhir` 1.5.0 join the profile package set.
