- The HL7 v2 face evaluates the guide's `IF CX.4 IN` identifier registry condition. A CX.4 with no namespace id and an `ISO` or `UUID` universal id writes `urn:oid:` or `urn:uuid:` as the identifier system with no lookup. Any other assigning authority is searched as an identifier `NamingSystem` on the terminology server (`GET NamingSystem?kind=identifier&value=<authority>`), and the one registration's `uri` becomes the system. A PID-3 authority no `NamingSystem` registers, or more than one does, is answered `AE` at PID-3 naming it, and a refused search `AR`. `ferrobridge-term` gains `Client::identifier_systems` behind its new `naming-system` feature.
