- `ferrobridge-term` speaks FHIR R5 and the R6 ballot (6.0.0-ballot5) beside STU3, R4 and R4B, each request written and each answer read through that release's generated `fhir-types` contract, so the `$translate` renames (`sourceCode`, `targetSystem`, `sourceSystem`, `sourceVersion`) and `match.relationship` come from the generated tables. It also sends `CodeSystem/$validate-code`, the instance-level forms, an inline `ValueSet` to `$expand`, and the inputs `activeOnly`, `excludeNested`, `displayLanguage` and `conceptMapVersion`; invokes an operation in the `GET` form where every input is primitive (`Config::with_form`); sends any request through `Client::send`; and writes and reads requests without its transport (`invocation::Invocation::new`, `invocation::answer`) for a caller that sends through its own.
