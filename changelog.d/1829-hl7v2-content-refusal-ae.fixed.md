- The HL7 v2 face answers `AE` when the CDR refuses a message's contribution for its content (`400` or `422`), and `AR` only when the CDR fails or refuses the bridge's credentials, so a sender no longer resends a message the CDR will always refuse (HL7 v2.5.1 §2.9.2.2). The face judges the status the CDR answered, which the FHIR facade's status table may render as a `500` or `502` of its own.
