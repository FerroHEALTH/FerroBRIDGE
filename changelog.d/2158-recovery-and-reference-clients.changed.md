- The inbound recovery calls (a webhook source's `redeliver` call, its `reconcile` polls and a FHIR Subscription source's `$events` recovery) and the facade's reference fetches now go through the family's outbound client, the one the audit feed and the outbound FHIR lane use. A recovery client is built once per source version and credential and again when the credential's secret changes, in place of a new client on every call; it keeps the 30-second limit and the single try, and it now follows no redirect, so a source that answers a recovery call with a redirect gets the call refused with that status.
