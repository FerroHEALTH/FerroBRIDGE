- `ferrobridge-term` 0.1.146 breaks its public API. The request types moved from `outcome` to `request`. `Validation.value_set_url` is `Validation.value_set` (a `request::Target`, built by `Validation::new`), `Expansion.value_set_url` is `Expansion.value_set` (a `request::ValueSetSource`), and `Subsumption.system` is `Subsumption.code_system`. `Request::Lookup` carries `display_language`, `Translation` carries `concept_map_version`, and `Expansion` and `Validation` carry the new inputs. On the answers, `ValidateOutcome::Valid` and `Invalid` both carry `display`, `outcome` and `details`, `TranslateOutcome::Translated` and `NoMatch` both carry `matches` and `used_maps`, `LookupOutcome::Found` carries `definition`, `Expanded` carries `parameters` and `unclosed`, and `Match` and `error::Issue` carry the further elements of the answer. Pattern matches that name every field and struct literals of these types change; `..` keeps a match compiling.
