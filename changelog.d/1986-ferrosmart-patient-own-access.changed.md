- Behind FerroSMART, a patient's own token is now the person's own access. Under `ferrohealth-smart` 0.31 the `ferrosmart` preset reads such a token as the patient: `fhirUser` is `Patient/<x>`, the `patient` claim is `x`, and the scopes are `patient/` grants alone. The facade's access record carries the binding `self` for it. The onward lane exchanges it at the `patients` issuer when one is declared, and otherwise at the `persons` issuer. Before, the lane refused a patient at a `persons` issuer. A professional who holds `patient/` scopes is unchanged. To keep the earlier reading, set `fhir_user_patient = false` on the FerroSMART `[[auth.issuer]]` table.
