- The change feed keeps its state in the PostgreSQL state store, so the bridge runs its change-feed consumers on several replicas. Each reader (the events lane, the incremental OMOP load, the outbound FHIR lane) has one leader across replicas through a lease in the store, renewed every third of the new `[change_feed] lease` (default `30s`); the other replicas stand by and report the new consumer state `standby`, and once a leader's lease runs out another replica takes the reader over and resumes at the first event the watermark does not cover. The watermarks of the `aql` source, the parked events and their poisoned-pass counts are store rows, and every write a follower makes carries its lease generation, so a former leader moves no watermark. An operator settles a parked event by a retry or a discard with a reason: `POST /operator/events/{consumer}/parked/{id}/retry` (`202`) or `…/discard` (`204`), with the reason in the `ferrobridge-reason` header, and the row keeps who decided, the decision and the reason, which `GET /operator/events` shows on each parked event; the console's Events page gives each parked event a reason field and Retry and Discard buttons. Upgrade note: `[change_feed]` now needs a `[state]` section; `change_feed.watermarks`, `change_feed.dead_letters` and `POST /operator/events/{consumer}/parked/{id}/replay` are removed with no replacement key, and the `redb` files they named are no longer read, so a reader on the `aql` source starts again where `start` says.
