- `fhir-types` 0.6.0 declares a nested `subproperty` part inside `CodeSystem/$lookup` `property.subproperty` for R4, R4B, R5 and R6, with the same `code`, `value` and `description` parts as its parent. The terminology ecosystem IG's ICD-11 page (`icd-11.md`, Properties) carries a cluster member's own `postcoordination` subproperties there, one level down. The new public field breaks a struct literal of `CodeSystemLookupResponsePropertySubproperty`, so a consumer that builds one adds it. The workspace still builds on the published `fhir-types` 0.4.0 until `ihe-atna` and `ihe-iti` move.
