- The facade no longer pays a state-store round trip for every identity lookup since the identity map moved into PostgreSQL. One written entry records its internal id, binding, identifiers and consumed source in one statement. Rows that are never changed (a person's EHR, an EHR's person, an internal id and its binding) are read through a bounded in-memory copy that serves only while the replica's `LISTEN` session is connected. The new `[state] identity_copy_entries` key (default `10000`, `0` keeps none) sets the copy's size.
