- The access-record lane keeps its audit outbox in the PostgreSQL state store. Each BALP `AuditEvent` is committed to `audit_outbox` before the answer, and every replica runs an `ihe-atna` forwarder over the one outbox that claims the oldest unclaimed record for a lease with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, deletes it once the Audit Record Repository answers `2xx` and moves a refused one to `audit_quarantine`, so two replicas never send one record and a replica that stops after it admitted an access loses no record. Recording still fails closed: an access is refused with `503` before the CDR is called while the outbox does not answer. Upgrade note: `[audit]` now needs `[state]`; `audit.spool_dir`, `audit.max_records`, `audit.max_size` and `audit.write_timeout` are removed with no replacement key, and records left in an old spool directory are not read, so let a running bridge drain its spool before the upgrade.
