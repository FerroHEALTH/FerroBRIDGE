- With `[inbound]` on, the HL7 v2 face stores the patient of each ADT patient trigger in the inbound intake before it answers: A01, A04, A05 and A08 (IHE ITI-8) and A28 and A31 (IHE ITI-30) as an upsert of the minimised `Patient`, and A40 and A47 as the source's merge statement (MRG-1 replaced by PID-3), which the inbound handler forwards to the identity source as a Resolve Duplicate. The event is keyed by MSH-10 and stamped by EVN-2, or MSH-7 without it; a repeat changes nothing and an older stamp is kept as superseded. The record travels sealed under `[state.seal]` and is cleared once applied, once its parked item is discarded, or after the new `[inbound] parked_body_retention` (default `7d`). `AA` follows the store's commit; a store failure answers `AE`.
