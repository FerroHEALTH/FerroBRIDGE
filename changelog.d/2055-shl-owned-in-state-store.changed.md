- SMART Health Links, SMART App State and the SMART launch `Task`s live in the PostgreSQL state store, which every replica shares, in place of their `redb` files. Each change of a link holds its row, so two replicas behind one address serve a file location once, count the passcode attempts and the answers across both, and refuse a revoked link alike (SMART Health Cards and Links 1.0.0); the expiry sweep runs on one replica at a time. A link's manifest id, its handle and each location token are stored as their SHA-256, and the handle is also sealed under a `[state.seal]` key so its owner can list it. A conditional write of App State or a launch `Task` holds the resource's row, so two replicas racing on one version cannot both win. Upgrade note: `[shl] store`, `[facade.app_state] store` and `[facade.launch_task] store` are removed; the links need `[state]` and `[state.seal]`, App State and launch `Task`s need `[state]`, and the old `redb` files are no longer read.
