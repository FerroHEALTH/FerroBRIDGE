- The bridge can cache the pseudonyms the identity source answers over ITI-83. The new `[identity_source.cache]` table turns it on. The cache serves only while the deployment's PMIR subscription to the identity source's feed is active, and drops what it held when the subscription stops being active. Each ITI-93 message received at `POST /pmir/feed` forgets, on every replica, the identifiers of the patients it updated, and every pseudonym when it carries a merge or a delete. The receiver admits only the access tokens of the clients `feed_clients` lists, so the cache needs `[auth]` and `[state]`. Under `[audit]`, the subscription's ITI-94 exchanges are recorded as the Subscriber's audit profiles fix them (#2388).
