- The facade serves the remaining draft EEHRxF Annex X entry groups through `eehrxf`'s Annex X project, over published CKM archetypes the EHDS record template now holds: EHDSAlert as `flag-patient-eu-core` from `EVALUATION.precaution.v1` (the CKM publishes no alert archetype), EHDSAdvanceDirective as `consent-eu-eps` from `EVALUATION.advance_care_directive.v2` with its document as the EHDSAttachment, EHDSBodyStructure as `bodyStructure-eu-core` from `CLUSTER.anatomical_location.v1`, EHDSCarePlan as `carePlan-eu-hdr`, EHDSCurrentPregnancyStatus and EHDSPregnancyHistory as the IPS pregnancy status and outcome profiles, EHDSTravelHistory as `observation-travel-eu-eps`, EHDSEncounter as `encounter-eu-hdr` from the encounter composition, and EHDSMedicationAdministration as `medicationAdministration-eu-hdr` with its EHDSAdministeredDosage over a template of its own. A `Consent` search without `scope` answers the access directives and the mapped advance directives as one searchset, paged across both, a `scope` of `patient-privacy` or `adr` reaches the one it names, the access directives' search takes `scope` in R4, and a `Consent` read reaches the lane its id belongs to. Two contexts of one resource type may share a template when each starts at an archetype of its own.
