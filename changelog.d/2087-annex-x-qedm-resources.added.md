- The facade serves seven data groups of draft EEHRxF Annex X as individual resources (draft act Art 7(2)) through `eehrxf`'s new Annex X project, `eehrxf::mappings::ANNEX_X`: allergies and intolerances, conditions, procedures, immunisations, medication use with the medication it references, and simple observations, each with its HL7 Europe Base and Core 2.0.1 profile (FHIR R4 `bodyweight` for a body weight) and the IHE QEDm 3.0.0 PCC-44 searches of its option declared beside its context. `Project::searches` names the search declarations a project ships, and `conformance/eehrxf-annex-x` counts the groups served out of 33.
