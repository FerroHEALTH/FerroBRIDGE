- The European interoperability component maps non-laboratory medical test reports from openEHR to the `EHDSMedicalTestReport` of draft EEHRxF Annex VIII (initiative 17512, 2026-11-23). A new shipped `test-report` FHIRconnect project, over FerroBRIDGE's template of the CKM `OBSERVATION.spirometry_result.v2` and `OBSERVATION.ecg_result.v1`, writes a spirometry or electrocardiogram result as an R4 `DiagnosticReport` (LOINC 18759-1 or 11524-6) in a document that carries the Annex III metadata (`eehrxf::mappings::TEST_REPORT`, `eehrxf::document::test_report`). No HL7 Europe guide profiles such a report, so the document claims the R4 base definitions, and `eehrxf::crosswalk::test_report` records each Annex VIII element against the R4 element that carries it, or none.
