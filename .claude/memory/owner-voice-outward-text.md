When drafting a forum post or a message the owner will send as themselves,
write it the way a person writes to colleagues. Use short plain paragraphs,
an informal opening and close, and at most one short list. No bold labels,
no stacked bullets, no "what is our own design" sections, and no formal
spec-citation style.

**Why:** the owner rejected the first Discourse topic and the note to Ian
(2026-11-14) as reading like AI: too structured and too formal.

**How to apply:** keep spec detail to what a reader needs to follow, link
the BMM and the write-up for the rest, and save the result where the owner
asks (their Downloads folder). Related: the writing-style rule.

When such a post cites openEHR specifications, link the development branch
(`specifications.openehr.org/releases/<component>/development/...`), not
`latest`: the owner treats development as the current text (2026-11-14).
Strengthen an argument with earlier Discourse threads and quotes from
openEHR's own authors, using Discourse's `[quote="user, post:N, topic:T"]`
form.
