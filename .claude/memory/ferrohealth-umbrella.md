**FerroHEALTH** is the family (mother) name; FerroBRIDGE is one product in it, beside FerroEHR, FerroTERM, FerroCHART, FerroFED and the planned FerroPIX, FerroSMART, FerroSYS, FerroTASK. Cadasto B.V. is the manufacturer of every product. The family site is <https://ferrohealth.eu> (repo FerroHEALTH/FerroHEALTH); each product documents itself on its own domain.

**Why:** owner 2026-10-08: "not everything is only for FerroEHR… it's about the bundled products". Tracked as FerroHEALTH/FerroHEALTH#37 (a family mdBook) and FerroHEALTH/FerroEHR#3701.

**How to apply:** before writing a page in FerroBRIDGE's book, ask whether it is about FerroBRIDGE alone. Material about the manufacturer (post-market, vulnerability handling, advisories, support period, licensing), about the EHR system FerroEHR and FerroBRIDGE form together under the EHDS (FerroBRIDGE ships its interoperability component), or about the CRA's manufacturer side belongs in the FerroHEALTH book; this book links there. FerroBRIDGE-specific pages stay here.
